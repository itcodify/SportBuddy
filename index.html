💡 Term of the Day: Deuce

In tennis, "Deuce" is a score of 40-40. Think of it like a tiebreaker baseline: from here, a player must win two consecutive points to win the game. It prevents games from ending on a lucky fluke!

What it means:

An attacker cannot hang out behind all defenders waiting for a pass. Think of it like cherry-picking: you must have at least two opponent players (usually the goalie and one defender) between you and their goal when the ball is passed to you.

💡 Simple Analogy: It's like standing at the classroom door waiting for the teacher to throw you a cheat sheet; you must step inside the room with everyone else first.

⚽ Soccer

Penalty Box

This large box (18 yards) defines the zone where the goalkeeper is allowed to use their hands. If a defender commits a foul inside this box, the other team gets a direct shot at the goal from the penalty spot (12 yards away).

💡 Analogy: It's the goalkeeper's "safety dome" and the danger zone for defenders. Inside here, defenders must play carefully!
